03 ColophonHow soft is madeExperiment 05 · 2026

        
Every letter is a bag of particles that remembers its shape.

        

          

            
01 Marching squares

            

              Each glyph is drawn once with Canvas2D in Bricolage Grotesque 800. Rust walks the alpha bitmap with marching squares,
              links crossings into closed loops, simplifies them with Douglas–Peucker and resamples to even spacing — outer rings and
              holes (the O keeps its O). 
            

          

          

            
02 Shape matching

            

              Müller et al. 2005. Every substep finds the best-fit rotation of the rest shape (a 2D polar decomposition is one atan2),
              blends in a volume-normalised linear transform, and pulls particles toward it. Stiffness is the “jelly ↔ rubber” slider.
            

          

          

            
03 Gas pressure

            

              An area constraint on the signed area of all rings keeps letters puffy when squashed and lets you inflate them. Push the
              pressure to the top and they protest. Double-click and they deflate like party balloons, then re-inflate.
            

          

          

            
04 Collisions

            

              Particles are tested against other glyphs with an even-odd inside test and nearest edge, pushed out with mass-split
              corrections and Coulomb friction. The poster’s info blocks are real rounded obstacles, sent to Rust as rects.
            

          

          

            
05 Rust → WebAssembly

            

              Six substeps per frame, positions read zero-copy from linear memory. 
            

          

          

            
06 Renderer

            

               Rest-pose glyphs are ear-clipped once (holes bridged); the deforming mesh reads Rust
              positions each frame. WGSL draws a soft shadow, a thick ink outline as a miter strip, the fill with halftone and a
              squash-dependent gloss, then a lit rim.
